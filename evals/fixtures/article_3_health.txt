Pathos Health today secured European CE mark regulatory certification for its clinical trial verification platform following an 18-month evaluation cycle. The Cambridge-headquartered healthcare intelligence provider demonstrated 99.4% precision in detecting discrepancies across 45,000 trial records during validation trials with University College London. Chief Medical Officer Dr Aris Thorne confirmed that the autonomous verification suite reduces compliance review cycles from 14 days to under 4 hours for pharmaceutical sponsors. The company raised 8.5 million euros in Series A funding led by Nordic Health Capital, bringing total capital raised to 12.2 million euros since inception in 2023. Commercial deployment will launch on November 1, 2026 across 12 partner hospitals in Germany, France, and Switzerland. Pathos plans to submit for United States FDA clearance in early 2027 while expanding its clinical data science team in Oxford by 25 research specialists. Independent biopharma auditors verified that automated protocol validation eliminates over 85% of transcription errors prior to regulatory submission. The platform integrates seamlessly with existing electronic data capture systems used across Phase II and Phase III global studies. Clinical researchers from University College London praised the system for establishing transparent audit trails for every evidentiary claim.
