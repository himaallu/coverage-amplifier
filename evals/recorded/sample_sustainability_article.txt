Financial News Syndicate — London.
Pathos EcoTechnologies announced today that its carbon accounting protocol has achieved ISO 14064 compliance following an audit by Bureau Veritas. The London-based climate analytics firm stated that its automated verification system reduced carbon reporting overhead by 70% across 60 multinational enterprises during pilot operations. Chief Sustainability Officer Elena Rostova reported that client firms saved an average of £1.4 million in annual compliance fines while verifying over 3.2 million metric tons of Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions.

The company also disclosed £6.8 million in new seed financing from GreenVentures UK and Horizon Europe. Pathos EcoTechnologies plans to expand its engineering hub in Manchester with 18 new climate data engineers by the third quarter of 2026. However, environmental watchdogs have cautioned that third-party validation of Scope 3 supply chain emissions remains challenging, noting that automated platforms must undergo rigorous independent scrutiny before being used for binding corporate disclosures. Pathos confirmed it will publish an open methodology paper in October 2026 to ensure full transparency across all reported carbon metrics.

The initiative comes amid tightening European Union Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive mandates which enforce penalties of up to 5% of global turnover for inaccurate environmental reporting. With commercial operations expanding across the Nordic region and North America, Pathos aims to position its verification engine as the industry standard for auditable sustainability data.
